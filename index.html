Who we are

A technology program built for young learners

THE RIJEE TECH is a Kenyan educational technology program focused on empowering children and teenagers with computer knowledge, coding, robotics, and problem-solving skills. Our programs are designed to be simple, visual, and engaging — giving learners a confident first step into technology, and giving parents and guardians a program they can trust.

Why it matters

Why technology skills matter for young people

Technology now shapes how young people learn, create, and solve problems. Building this literacy early gives them tools they can carry into any field.

Creative problem solving

Coding and robotics teach young learners to break big problems into small, manageable steps.

Logical thinking

Structured, hands-on activities build reasoning skills that carry over into every subject.

Innovation mindset

Learners are encouraged to experiment, build, and see technology as something they can shape.

How learning works

A simple path from first trial to steady progress
