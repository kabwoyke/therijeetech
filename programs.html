Our programs

Two beginner-friendly ways to start

Both programs are designed for older kids and teenagers aged 10–17, and start with a free trial class so every learner can find their fit.

Scratch Coding for Beginners

Introduces young learners to programming through Scratch — a simple, visual, and engaging way of exploring coding. Students snap together colourful blocks to build stories, games, and animations, learning core programming concepts without needing to type a single line of code.

Robotics for Beginners

Introduces students to robotics concepts and encourages practical problem-solving, creativity, technology skills, and innovation. Learners explore how robotics components work together through guided, hands-on activities suited to beginners.
