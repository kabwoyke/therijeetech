About us

About THE RIJEE TECH

THE RIJEE TECH is focused on exposing young people to computer knowledge and practical technology skills. We work with children and teenagers to build a confident, hands-on foundation in coding and robotics — skills that support learning and creativity across many areas of life.

Our mission

To enlighten the young society on computer knowledge, increasing their skills and encouraging innovation in different economic areas.

Building skills for the future

We see computer knowledge as a foundation young people can build on for years to come — supporting how they learn, create, and solve problems, whatever path they choose next.

Who we serve

Older kids and teens, aged 10–17

Our programs are designed specifically for this age group, using beginner-friendly methods that build confidence step by step — whether a student is completely new to technology or already curious about it.

Nairobi, and across Kenya
